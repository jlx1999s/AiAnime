# Motion Prompt 模板

[任务指令]
    读取 sequence-board-prompt.md，为每个 Beat Anchor 生成一组动态提示词：
    - 1 个关键帧动态提示词（对应九宫格该格）
    - 4 个四宫格动态提示词（对应四宫格展开）
    共 9 组，每组 5 个，共 45 个 Motion Prompt

---

# 动态提示词（Motion Prompts）

## 格1：神秘快递 (The Delivery)

### 关键帧
1-0: Slow dolly in on messy room, exhausted man slumped in chair, subtle breathing motion, flickering monitor light, cinematic shadows.

### 四宫格展开
1-1: Pan right across messy desk with takeout boxes, dust particles floating in monitor light, gloomy atmosphere.
1-2: Close up on Li Daoxuan's face, rubbing temples, eyes blinking slowly, expression of deep fatigue.
1-3: Static shot of large cardboard box in doorway, ominous presence, low key lighting, high contrast.
1-4: Extreme close up on yellow sticky note reading "Happy Birthday", paper texture visible, slight air movement.

---

## 格2：开箱 (Unboxing)

### 关键帧
2-0: Over-the-shoulder shot, hands slicing tape with utility knife, cardboard flaps springing open, revealing glass surface.

### 四宫格展开
2-1: Close up of utility knife blade cutting through tape, sharp focus, slow motion, debris flying.
2-2: Hands peeling back cardboard layers, revealing internal glass structure, light glinting off glass.
2-3: Top-down view, box fully opened, revealing miniature desert landscape inside, realistic texture.
2-4: Li Daoxuan's face illuminated by light from the box, expression shifts from tired to intrigued, eyebrows raised.

---

## 格3：微观求生 (Micro Survival)

### 关键帧
3-0: Macro lens shot through glass, tiny ancient girl digging in dry soil, dust rising, desperate movements.

### 四宫格展开
3-1: Extreme close up of magnifying glass lens, distorted view of miniature village, rack focus to interior.
3-2: Tiny girl's hands scratching dry earth, pulling out dry root, soil crumbling, high detail texture.
3-3: Girl chewing on dry root, facial muscles moving, expression of relief and pain, cinematic lighting.
3-4: Wide shot of miniature ruined village inside glass, yellow sand blowing, dead trees swaying slightly.

---

## 格4：惊变 (The Realization)

### 关键帧
4-0: Medium shot of Li holding phone, sudden head turn to the box, eyes widening, tense atmosphere.

### 四宫格展开
4-1: Li talking on phone, lips moving naturally, hand gesturing slightly, confused expression.
4-2: Close up on phone screen pressed to ear, subtle camera shake indicating tension.
4-3: Rack focus from Li's face to the glass box in background, ominous vibe.
4-4: Quick zoom in on the box glass, blurring edges, suggesting sound coming from within.

---

## 格5：山贼突袭 (The Raid)

### 关键帧
5-0: Micro cinematic shot, miniature bandits on horses galloping, dust clouds billowing, swords waving, dynamic tracking shot.

### 四宫格展开
5-1: Low angle micro shot, horse hooves trampling ground, dust flying, camera shaking.
5-2: Bandit waving tiny sword, mouth opening in shout, fire light reflecting on metal.
5-3: Villagers running in panic, chaotic movement, smoke rising from burning huts.
5-4: High angle view of village, fires starting, bandits encircling area, chaotic motion.

---

## 格6：惨剧发生 ( The Tragedy)

### 关键帧
6-0: Slow motion, bandit slashing sword, red liquid splattering, mother figure falling, dramatic lighting.

### 四宫格展开
6-1: Close up of bandit's face, savage expression, shouting motion, sweat glistening.
6-2: Mother pushing girl behind her, desperate protective gesture, fabric rustling.
6-3: Sword impact (implied), camera jolts, red splatter on ground, tragic atmosphere.
6-4: Girl screaming, mouth wide open, tears flowing, body shaking, extreme emotional close up.

---

## 格7：绝望逼近 (Impending Doom)

### 关键帧
7-0: Low angle looking up at bandit, looming shadow over girl, bandit stepping forward, terrifying scale.

### 四宫格展开
7-1: Bandit stepping over body, heavy footsteps, boots kicking dust, slow approach.
7-2: Girl backing away against wall, trembling violently, eyes wide with terror.
7-3: Bandit raising bloody sword, slow deliberate motion, glint of steel.
7-4: Li's eye seen through the glass background, huge and out of focus, watching the scene.

---

## 格8：巨手降临 (Divine Intervention)

### 关键帧
8-0: Looking up from village, glass ceiling lifts, massive hand breaking through clouds, god-like scale, epic lighting.

### 四宫格展开
8-1: Macro view of glass lid lifting, sudden flood of bright light, shadows shifting rapidly.
8-2: Clouds in the box swirling, giant fingers piercing through, atmospheric turbulence.
8-3: Bandit looking up, head tilting back, expression changing from smug to horror.
8-4: Giant hand descending, palm open, blocking the sun, casting massive shadow over village.

---

## 格9：神罚 (The Flick)

### 关键帧
9-0: Giant finger flicking, shockwave visibly distorting air, bandit launching backward, explosive speed.

### 四宫格展开
9-1: Li's face stone cold, focus on middle finger building tension, muscles tightening.
9-2: Finger releasing snap, air rippling, extreme slow motion contact with bandit.
9-3: Bandit and horse disintegrating/blurring into motion lines, flying uncontrollably.
9-4: Impact on glass wall, red splatter, body crumpling, camera shake, heavy dust settling.
