# Motion Prompt 模板

[任务指令]
    读取 sequence-board-prompt.md，为每个 Beat Anchor 生成一组动态提示词：
    - 1 个关键帧动态提示词（对应九宫格该格）
    - 展开分镜对应的动态提示词

---

## 格1：【社畜的至暗时刻】

### 关键帧
1-0: Slow dolly in on man curled in chair, blue monitor light flickering on face, subtle breathing, lonely atmosphere, cinematic lighting.

### 展开
1-1: Static wide shot, faint blue light pulsing from screen, figure motionless in darkness, dust motes floating, oppressive silence.
1-2: Over-the-shoulder shot, dolly in towards screen, chat windows popping up rapidly, red notifications flashing, back muscles tense up.
1-3: Frontal medium shot, hand mechanically lifts fork to mouth, chewing slowly, eyes unblinking and hollow, dark circles under eyes, cold lighting.
1-4: Close up, chewing stops, head turns slightly right, eyes shift focus from screen to off-screen object, expression changes to subtle confusion.

---

## 格2：【箱中微尘】

### 关键帧
2-0: Side profile close up, face pressing against glass, eye moving to scan inside, reflection of screen on glass surface.

### 展开
2-1: Extreme close up side view, giant eye blinks behind glass wall, breath fogging up the glass slightly, mysterious vibe.
2-2: Macro shot, rack focus from blurred foreground to tiny figure on yellow earth, figure crawling slowly, high texture detail.
2-3: Macro side angle, trembling hands digging into dry soil, pulling out dry root, dust particles rising, shallow depth of field.
2-4: Macro frontal close up, frantically stuffing root into mouth, eyes darting left and right in panic, dirt falling from lips.

---

## 格3：【血色荒原】

### 关键帧
3-0: High angle, tiny horses charging across yellow ground, dust billowing, sudden chaotic movement.

### 展开
3-1: Top-down pan shot following bandits, horses galloping fast, dust clouds rising, mother and girl huddle together in fear.
3-2: Medium shot, mother throws body over girl, bandit swings blade down violently, motion blur on weapon, dramatic tension.
3-3: Frozen moment, blade strikes, blood sprays upwards against yellow soil, high contrast, visceral impact.
3-4: Extreme close up, girl's mouth opens in silent scream, tears streaming, blood dripping onto face, camera shakes slightly.

---

## 格4：【刀锋之下】

### 关键帧
4-0: Low angle looking up, bandit on horse looming over camera, shadow casts over face, menacing grin.

### 展开
4-1: Low angle POV, dark shadow creeps across girl's face, bandit silhouette blocks the sun, horse stomps hoof.
4-2: Extreme macro close up, rusty blade tip moves closer to lens, sharp focus on metal texture, background blurred.
4-3: High angle close up, girl squeezes eyes shut tight, body trembles violently, tears roll down dirty cheek, helpless.
4-4: Low angle Dutch angle, bandit's face distorts in maniacal laughter, yellow teeth exposed, saliva flying, intense evil.

---

## 格5：【天怒】

### 关键帧
5-0: Face close up, eyes snap open wide, pupils contract, expression shifts from numbness to pure rage, veins pop.

### 展开
5-1: Extreme close up, eyebrows furrow deeply, teeth gritted, blue screen light reflection in eyes replaced by inner fire.
5-2: Side shot, abruptly stands up, chair flies backward and hits floor, body lunges forward aggressively, fast motion.
5-3: Wide angle low shot, huge hand reaches out and blocks ceiling light, casting massive shadow over room, dramatic lighting.
5-4: Close up, fingers grip edge of glass lid, lid lifts up forcefully, light floods in, dust particles dance in turbulence.

---

## 格6：【苍穹破碎】

### 关键帧
6-0: Low angle looking up, ceiling dissolves into blinding white light, clouds disperse rapidly, god rays shine down.

### 展开
6-1: Upward pan, artificial sky tears open, blinding external light pours in like a waterfall, atmospheric turbulence.
6-2: Low angle, giant hand descends slowly through clouds, skin texture visible, fingerprints like canyons, megalophobia aesthetic.
6-3: Wide shot, tiny horses rear up in panic, bandits look up in shock, chaotic formation breaking, camera pulls back.
6-4: Medium shot, giant shadow rapidly engulfs the bandit leader, smile vanishes, expression turns to frozen horror.

---

## 格7：【神之弹指】

### 关键帧
7-0: Extreme close up, giant thumb presses against middle finger, skin turns white from pressure, tension building.

### 展开
7-1: Macro shot of fingers, muscles tensing, potential energy accumulating, nail edge sharp against background.
7-2: POV shot through giant fingers, tiny bandit trying to turn horse to flee, focus locked on target.
7-3: Low angle close up on bandit, eyes wide open, mouth agape, frozen in terror, reflection of giant finger in eye.
7-4: Side profile macro, middle finger slides slightly on thumb, friction visible, time stands still, anticipation.

---

## 格8：【降维打击】

### 关键帧
8-0: Finger snaps forward, air distortion, white sonic boom ring appears, extreme motion blur, explosive impact.

### 展开
8-1: Action capture, finger releases, shockwave distorts the air, high velocity motion, impact frame.
8-2: Super slow motion, giant finger connects with bandit, body and horse disintegrate on impact, debris flies, physics destruction.
8-3: Top down wide shot, circular dust wave expands rapidly from impact point, knocking over other horses, camera shakes violently.
8-4: Static shot, dust settling, deep fingerprint crater remains in ground, smoke rising, absolute silence.

---

## 格9：【直视神颜】

### 关键帧
9-0: Medium shot, girl shakily stands up from dust, looking around in confusion, debris falling in background.

### 展开
9-1: Medium shot, girl rises from ruins, coughing, clothes flapping in settling wind, eyes filled with disbelief.
9-2: Close up, head tilts back slowly, eyes widen, gaze travels upward into the light, expression shifts to awe.
9-3: Low angle wide shot, giant face fills the entire sky/ceiling, looking down with complex expression of pity and power.
9-4: Extreme wide shot, tiny girl kneeling below, giant face above, light rays connecting them, epic scale, religious composition.
