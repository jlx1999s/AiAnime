# Motion Prompt 模板

[任务指令]
    读取 sequence-board-prompt.md，为每个 Beat Anchor 生成一组动态提示词：
    - 1 个关键帧动态提示词（对应九宫格该格）
    - 展开分镜对应的动态提示词

---

## 格1：神颜俯瞰

### 关键帧
1-0: Low angle tilt up, ceiling cracks open revealing blinding light beams, tyndall effect, dust particles floating, dramatic atmosphere, cinematic lighting.

### 展开
1-1: Low angle looking up, ceiling splits open slowly, intense light beams pierce through darkness, dust swirling in light rays, cinematic lighting, ominous mood.
1-2: Extreme close-up low angle, giant eye pupil constricts and scans left to right, realistic eye texture, intimidating gaze, ominous atmosphere, macro photography.
1-3: High angle static shot, tiny figures on ground suddenly freeze, weapons dropping from hands, dust settling, sense of shock, miniature effect.
1-4: Low angle wide shot, massive silhouette looms over camera obscuring the light, slow push in, shadow engulfs the landscape, epic scale, high contrast.

---

## 格2：只手遮天

### 关键帧
2-0: Low angle, giant hand descends rapidly from sky, shadow grows larger on ground, wind picking up, intense motion blur, dynamic composition.

### 展开
2-1: Low angle, massive palm descending quickly, fingers like mountains, debris and sand rising up due to air pressure, darkening sky, motion blur.
2-2: Ground level shot, giant hand slams into ground, explosion of dust and debris, shockwave spreads outward, camera shakes violently, chaotic motion.
2-3: Medium shot, female character blown backward by wind force, curling up on ground, flying sand and rocks, chaotic camera movement, grey dusty atmosphere.
2-4: High angle wide shot, giant hand rests motionless on ground, dust slowly settling around fingers, stillness, cinematic texture, eerie silence.

---

## 格3：指缝神迹

### 关键帧
3-0: High angle, giant hand slowly lifts up, revealing a deep palm print in earth, light floods back in, dust swirling, dramatic reveal.

### 展开
3-1: Top down view, giant hand slowly rising and moving away, shadow retreating, sunlight gradually illuminating the ground, heavy atmosphere.
3-2: Macro close-up, slow pan over deep ground indentation, crushed metal and dark red textures in mud, realistic details, sharp focus on debris.
3-3: High angle zoom out, tiny female figure kneeling safely in the gap between finger indentations, surrounding ground is destroyed, dramatic lighting contrast.
3-4: Close-up on face, eyes slowly open, eyelashes fluttering, dirt on face, expression shifts from dazed to shocked, breathing heavily.

---

## 格4：狂热信徒

### 展开
4-0: Medium shot, character looks up at divine light, falls to knees, intense emotional expression, atmospheric lighting, slow motion.

### 展开
4-1: Side profile medium shot, character kneeling, slowly lifts head to look at light source above, divine light beam illuminates dirty face, hope and awe.
4-2: High angle, character violently bows head to ground, hands clawing into dirt, desperate body language, camera slight shake on impact.
4-3: Frontal close-up, head lifts up, blood on forehead, mouth moving rapidly in prayer, eyes wide with manic devotion, facial muscles twitching, intense focus.
4-4: Extreme wide shot, silhouette of tiny kneeling figure against backdrop of massive handprint crater, dust floating, cinematic composition, epic scale.

---

## 格5：打破次元

### 关键帧
5-0: Medium shot, man sits in chair, warm indoor lighting, looks at palm with confusion, rack focus from background to hand, realistic texture.

### 展开
5-1: Medium shot, man leans back in chair, rubbing temples, warm desk lamp lighting, background cluttered room, subtle camera drift.
5-2: Over-the-shoulder shot, man slowly raises left hand to light, palm turns upward, focus shifts to hand, hesitant movement.
5-3: Macro close-up on palm, thick dark red liquid oozing in skin texture, wet reflection under lamp light, hyper-realistic skin details, slight hand tremble.
5-4: Frontal close-up, pupils contract rapidly, sweat bead rolling down temple, expression transforms from confusion to pure horror, heavy breathing, dramatic lighting.
