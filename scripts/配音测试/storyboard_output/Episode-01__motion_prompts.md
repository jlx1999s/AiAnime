# Motion Prompt 模板

[任务指令]
    读取 sequence-board-prompt.md，为每个 Beat Anchor 生成一组动态提示词：
    - 1 个关键帧动态提示词（对应九宫格该格）
    - 4 个四宫格动态提示词（对应四宫格展开）
    共 9 组，每组 5 个，共 45 个 Motion Prompt

---

# 动态提示词（Motion Prompts）

## 格1：开箱 (Unboxing)

### 关键帧
1-0: Medium shot, Li Daoxuan lifts the cardboard box lid, a burst of warm golden light floods his face, dust particles dancing in the light beams, cinematic lighting, high contrast.

### 四宫格展开
1-1: Extreme close-up, sharp utility knife blade slices smoothly through clear tape, cold blue screen light reflects on the metal, shallow depth of field.
1-2: Over-the-shoulder shot, hands lifting the heavy cardboard flaps, warm yellow light erupts from within, illuminating the dark room, dynamic lighting shift.
1-3: Low angle medium shot, Li Daoxuan leans forward, eyes widen in amazement, mouth slightly opens, warm light washing over his face, subtle camera push in.
1-4: POV shot, looking down into the box, dust motes floating in shafts of light, miniature dry trees and tiny thatched houses visible, slight handheld camera movement.

---

## 格2：窥视饿殍 (Peeping at Starvation)

### 关键帧
2-0: Side profile, Li Daoxuan holding a large magnifying glass, looking down, glass surface reflecting a distorted miniature world, cinematic atmosphere.

### 四宫格展开
2-1: Side shot, hand lifts a large black magnifying glass into frame, adjusting position over the box, glass reflects ambient light, focus shifts to the lens.
2-2: POV through magnifying glass, optical spherical distortion at edges, blurry colors rack focus into a sharp image of a tiny human figure, chromatic aberration.
2-3: Macro close-up, dirty trembling hand stuffs dry grass root into cracked lips, chewing slowly, extreme detail on skin texture, heartbreaking realism.
2-4: Macro close-up, throat swallowing with difficulty, facial muscles tense up in pain, eyes tearing up, subtle camera shake to emphasize struggle.

---

## 格3：流寇突袭 (Bandit Raid)

### 关键帧
3-0: Low angle dynamic shot, bandit on horseback charging through dust, sword raised, miniature village background, motion blur, chaos and speed.

### 四宫格展开
3-1: Low angle ground view, small pebbles vibrating and jumping on the ground, background villagers freeze and turn heads, rack focus from ground to villagers.
3-2: Medium shot, warhorse bursts through thick yellow dust, bandit shouting with ferocious expression, debris flying, high shutter speed, impact frame.
3-3: High angle overhead shot, horse crashing into crowd, villagers scattering in panic, chaotic movement, camera shakes violently, motion blur.
3-4: Dutch angle low shot, bandit raises rusty steel blade high, sunlight glints sharply on the edge, ominous shadow cast over camera, freeze frame feel.

---

## 格4：母亲之死 (Mother's Death)

### 关键帧
4-0: Medium shot, mother shielding her daughter, bandit's shadow looming over them, dramatic lighting, tragic atmosphere, slow motion.

### 四宫格展开
4-1: Medium shot, mother desperately pushes girl behind her back, arms spreading wide, face filled with fear but determination, fast camera push in.
4-2: Side profile close-up, sword blade slashing down, impact frame, clothes tearing, sudden burst of blood mist, brutal and fast motion.
4-3: POV shot from low angle, mother's body goes limp and collapses slowly to the side, revealing the towering bandit on horse behind her, tragic slow motion.
4-4: Frontal close-up, Gao Yiye's face splattered with blood specks, mouth opens wide in a silent scream, pupils trembling, extreme emotional shock, camera dolly zoom (vertigo effect).

---

## 格5：死亡逼近 (Death Approaches)

### 关键帧
5-0: Low angle, bandit looming over camera with sword, terrified girl backing away on the ground, intense suspense, predator vs prey dynamic.

### 四宫格展开
5-1: Extreme close-up, bandit's rough tongue licks blood off the blade, yellow teeth exposed, eyes widening in manic excitement, creepy micro-movements.
5-2: Medium shot, horse turns around, hooves stomping, bandit's gaze locks onto the target below, camera tracks the turning motion.
5-3: High angle looking down, Gao Yiye crawling backward on hands and feet, back hits the wall, body shaking violently, dust rising from movement.
5-4: Low angle POV, bandit raises the butcher knife again, tip pointing directly at lens, blue sky in background, camera creates a sense of shrinking back.

---

## 格6：巨手降临 (Giant Hand Descends)

### 关键帧
6-0: Wide shot, a colossal human hand descends from the clouds above the miniature village, casting a massive shadow, surreal scale contrast, epic atmosphere.

### 四宫格展开
6-1: Medium shot, bandit stops mid-swing, looks up in confusion, sunlight rapidly dims to darkness, dramatic lighting change.
6-2: Looking up, massive realistic clouds are pushed aside physically, revealing a gigantic skin-textured ceiling, heavy atmosphere.
6-3: Wide angle, a titanic hand descends slowly from top frame, covering the sky, bandit and horse look like ants, shadow engulfs the village, slow epic movement.
6-4: Medium shot, bandit and horse frozen in place, horse kneeling in fear, giant fingertips hover just above their heads, absolute stillness.

---

## 格7：神之弹指 (God's Flick)

### 关键帧
7-0: Macro shot, giant fingers forming a flicking gesture, tension building in the muscles, miniature bandit in the background out of focus, anticipation.

### 四宫格展开
7-1: Side profile close-up, giant index finger curls against thumb, skin texture and fingerprints visible like landscapes, aiming carefully.
7-2: Rack focus shot, focus shifts from the deep grooves of the giant fingerprint in foreground to the terrified tiny bandit in background.
7-3: Extreme close-up, thumb nail turning white from pressure, skin stretching, air shimmering around the finger, maximum tension.
7-4: Side shot, finger releases, instant motion blur, explosive release of energy, shockwave visual effect, high velocity.

---

## 格8：降维打击 (Dimensional Strike)

### 关键帧
8-0: Tracking shot, bandit and horse flying through the air uncontrollably, motion blur, background passing rapidly, physics-defying impact.

### 四宫格展开
8-1: Frozen time shot, giant finger makes contact, armor crumples instantly, horse body deforms, shockwave ripples, extreme physics impact.
8-2: Fast tracking shot, bandit becomes a blur, flying horizontally across the miniature landscape, camera struggles to keep up, wind effects.
8-3: Side shot, body slams into the glass wall of the tank, violent impact, blood splatters on the glass surface, body twists unnaturally.
8-4: Static wide shot, distorted corpse slides slowly down the glass wall leaving a red streak, dust settles, silence, heavy atmosphere.

---

## 格9：指尖真血 (Real Blood)

### 关键帧
9-0: Close up, Li Daoxuan looking at his finger in horror, a drop of real blood on the fingertip, dark room, blue screen light, psychological thriller vibe.

### 四宫格展开
9-1: Medium shot, Li Daoxuan jerks his hand back violently, breathing heavily, chest rising and falling rapidly, camera handheld shake.
9-2: Close up, holding hand up to face, eyes squinting to inspect, blue monitor light illuminating the side of his face, focus hunting.
9-3: Macro extreme close-up, a smear of bright red blood clearly visible on the finger ridges, blood soaking into skin texture, sharp focus.
9-4: Frontal big close-up, Li Daoxuan's eyes wide open, pupils contract sharply (dolly zoom), expression of pure disbelief and terror, background fades to black.
