# Motion Prompt 模板

[任务指令]
    读取 sequence-board-prompt.md，为每个 Beat Anchor 生成一组动态提示词：
    - 1 个关键帧动态提示词（对应九宫格该格）
    - 展开分镜对应的动态提示词

---

## 格1：真血的铁锈味

### 关键帧
1-0: Cinematic medium shot, Li Daoxuan stands frozen in a modern room, staring at his palm, breathing heavily, cold blue lighting, slight handheld camera movement to suggest anxiety.

### 展开
1-1: POV close-up, hand trembling slightly in center frame, blood texture glistening under cold light, shallow depth of field, rack focus from blood to blurred background.
1-2: POV extreme close-up, thumb forcefully smears wet blood on palm, liquid texture spreading into skin lines, skin turning white from pressure, realistic friction motion.
1-3: Side profile close-up, hand jerks up towards nose rapidly, facial expression shifts from confusion to disgust, nose wrinkles, eyes widen in shock, micro-muscle twitching.
1-4: Medium shot, sudden whip pan as subject stumbles backward violently, knocking into chair, body recoiling in terror, eyes locked on the vivarium, heavy breathing motion.

## 格2：透镜下的悲剧

### 关键帧
2-0: Close-up side angle, large magnifying glass held against glass wall, reflections of the blue room moving across the curved lens surface, cinematic lighting contrast.

### 展开
2-1: Macro side shot, hand presses magnifying glass firmly against tank wall, slight adjustments in angle, reflection of cold blue room shifts on the glass surface, sharp contrast with warm interior light.
2-2: POV through lens, heavy optical distortion and chromatic aberration, image shifts from extreme blur to gradually finding focus on a miniature figure, searching camera movement.
2-3: Extreme macro close-up through lens, sharp focus locks on Gao Yiye's face, tears streaming down muddy cheeks, mouth opening in a silent scream, high detail texture, bokeh background.
2-4: Reverse shot, magnifying glass slowly lowers revealing Li's pale shocked face, eyes widening in disbelief, rack focus from the blurred lens in foreground to the face in background.

## 格3：世界观崩塌

### 关键帧
3-0: Wide shot, dark room illuminated by blue screen light, Li sits motionless in the ergonomic chair, dust particles floating in the light beams, oppressive atmosphere.

### 展开
3-1: Medium shot, body collapses heavily into the ergonomic chair, chair rolls backward slightly with momentum, arms flop down lifelessly, sudden loss of muscle tension.
3-2: Close-up, head tilts back slowly against headrest, eyes unfocused staring at ceiling, chest rising and falling rapidly, high contrast blue lighting splitting the face.
3-3: Tight close-up, hands grab messy hair violently, fingers digging into scalp, head shaking slightly in denial, teeth clenched, shadow obscuring eyes.
3-4: High angle wide shot, slow crane up, subject curled up small in the chair, surrounding darkness appearing to encroach on the center, screen light flickering weakly.

## 格4：现实的暴击

### 关键帧
4-0: Extreme close-up of computer monitor, pixels visible, digital glare, screen content static but light intensity fluctuating slightly.

### 展开
4-1: Macro screen shot, bright red notification icon pops up suddenly in the corner, pulsing animation, breaking the static wallpaper, sharp digital movement.
4-2: Screen capture view, mouse cursor moves erratically, comment section scrolls rapidly upwards, text blurring from speed, harsh white light, information overload.
4-3: Static macro shot, "Termination Notice" text stays fixed and sharp, mouse cursor hovers and trembles slightly over the bold text, cold clinical atmosphere.
4-4: Over-the-shoulder shot, screen turns black, reflection of Li's distorted face appears on the glass, slow dolly out, reflection looks ghostly and hollow.
