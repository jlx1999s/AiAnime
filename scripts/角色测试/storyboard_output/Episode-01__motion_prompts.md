# Motion Prompt 模板

[任务指令]
    读取 sequence-board-prompt.md，为每个 Beat Anchor 生成一组动态提示词：
    - 1 个关键帧动态提示词（对应九宫格该格）
    - 4 个四宫格动态提示词（对应四宫格展开）
    共 9 组，每组 5 个，共 45 个 Motion Prompt

---

# 动态提示词（Motion Prompts）

## 格1：深夜社畜

### 关键帧
1-0: Slow dolly in, Li Daoxuan collapses onto sofa, chest heaving with deep sigh, blue monitor light flickering on face, exhaustion, cinematic lighting.

### 四宫格展开
1-1: Medium shot, Daoxuan loosens tie with trembling hand, head tilts back, eyes closing slowly, messy apartment background.
1-2: Close up on face, heavy dark circles, blue light reflecting in tired eyes, subtle head shake, melancholic atmosphere.
1-3: Pan right across messy room, revealing instant noodle cups and papers, landing on Daoxuan's slumped figure, shadow play.
1-4: Low angle, Daoxuan stares blankly at ceiling, blinking slowly, ambient dust particles floating in blue light.

---

## 格2：神秘快递

### 关键帧
2-0: Static camera, Daoxuan walks heavily towards large box at door, dragging feet, shadow stretches across floor, suspenseful atmosphere.

### 四宫格展开
2-1: POV shot approaching the box, "Happy Birthday" text comes into focus, slight camera shake mimicking walking.
2-2: Medium shot, Daoxuan stands over the box, hands on hips, eyebrows furrowed in confusion, dim hallway light.
2-3: Low angle looking up from box, Daoxuan's silhouette looming over, hand reaching down towards camera.
2-4: Close up on box surface, texture of cardboard, hand touches the surface, dust motes dancing in light.

---

## 格3：废土开箱

### 关键帧
3-0: High angle, cardboard tears open revealing glass cover, warm yellow glow illuminates Daoxuan's surprised face, dynamic lighting change.

### 四宫格展开
3-1: Over-the-shoulder shot, cardboard flaps being pulled back violently, revealing the glass enclosure inside.
3-2: Close up on Daoxuan's face, eyes widen, warm orange light reflects on his skin, expression shifts from annoyance to shock.
3-3: Macro shot inside the box through glass, miniature desolate wasteland, dead trees, sand blowing, realistic texture.
3-4: Dolly in towards the glass, reflection of Daoxuan's face superimposed over the miniature yellow desert.

---

## 格4：微观窥视

### 关键帧
4-0: Extreme close up through magnifying glass, optical distortion, tiny girl (Yuniang) digging in soil, focus shifts from glass surface to girl.

### 四宫格展开
4-1: Side view, Daoxuan holding magnifying glass to eye, squinting, lens flare from the box's internal light.
4-2: Micro view through lens, Yuniang's tiny hand pulling out a dry grass root, shaking off dirt, high detail.
4-3: Micro view, Yuniang puts root in mouth, chewing desperately, ragged clothes fluttering in miniature wind.
4-4: Medium shot, Daoxuan lowers magnifying glass slightly, frowning, expression of pity and disbelief.

---

## 格5：异响惊变

### 关键帧
5-0: Medium shot, Daoxuan holding phone to ear, sudden freeze, eyes dart to the box, phone slips slightly in hand.

### 四宫格展开
5-1: Close up on phone screen, call timer running, Daoxuan's lips moving "Hello?", sudden pause.
5-2: Rack focus from Daoxuan's face to the landscape box in background, subtle vibration or buzzing visual effect.
5-3: Daoxuan's hand stiffens, fingers gripping phone tighter, head turns sharply towards the box.
5-4: Dolly zoom (vertigo effect) on Daoxuan, background stretches, emphasizing sudden realization and tension.

---

## 格6：山贼突袭

### 关键帧
6-0: Micro cinematic shot, miniature bandits charge into frame, horses kicking up dust, rusty swords swinging, camera follows movement.

### 四宫格展开
6-1: Low angle micro shot, wooden door kicked open, splinters flying, bandit silhouette framed in doorway.
6-2: High angle micro shot, bandits dragging Yuniang and mother across sand, struggle marks on ground, chaotic motion.
6-3: Close up on bandit leader's face (tiny scale), shouting, mouth moving, scar visible, savage expression.
6-4: Yuniang's mother kneeling, head banging on ground repeatedly, dust rising with each kowtow.

---

## 格7：惨剧发生

### 关键帧
7-0: Slow motion, bandit's sword swings down, blood sprays onto yellow sand, Daoxuan's giant face visible in sky (reflection) looking horrific.

### 四宫格展开
7-1: Micro shot, mother's body falls limp, red liquid pools rapidly on sand, body twitches slightly.
7-2: Daoxuan's POV looking down, hand covers mouth, eyes wide with shock, "giant" perspective.
7-3: Yuniang crawling towards mother's body, mouth open in silent scream, tears visible, heartbreaking atmosphere.
7-4: Bandit leader raises sword again, pointing at Yuniang, cold lighting, brutal realism.

---

## 格8：巨手降临

### 关键帧
8-0: Low angle looking up from inside box, glass lid lifts, massive wind storm created, giant hand descends from "heavens".

### 四宫格展开
8-1: External view, Daoxuan angrily throws glass lid aside, hair flying, intense anger, camera pushes in fast.
8-2: Internal view, sky turns dark as giant fingers break through clouds/atmosphere, lightning/thunder effect.
8-3: Bandit leader looks up, expression changes from arrogance to absolute terror, sword drops from hand.
8-4: Giant hand hovers over the village, shadow engulfing the bandits, dust swirling violently around giant fingers.

---

## 格9：神罚一指

### 关键帧
9-0: Extreme close up, giant finger flicks, shockwave ripple distorts air, bandit leader flies backward like a bullet.

### 四宫格展开
9-1: Side angle, finger tension builds, then releases, explosive motion, air bursts around the impact point.
9-2: Tracking shot following the bandit flying through air, smashing into rock wall, turning into red mist/paste.
9-3: Wide shot of village, remaining bandits frozen, looking up at the giant hand withdrawing, dust settling.
9-4: Close up on Daoxuan's finger tip, slight tremor, looking at faint blood stain, breathing heavily, rack focus to his eye.
